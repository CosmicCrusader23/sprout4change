Our work

Awareness is where action begins.

S4C connects sustainability knowledge with actions young people can try in everyday life, at school and with their communities.

Our activities are being developed in phases. The examples below describe the work we aim to support—not a list of projects already completed.

01

Awareness & Advocacy

We produce regular social media content that helps young people see sustainability in familiar places and choices.

  • Simple sustainable habits
  • Sustainability issues relevant to Hong Kong
  • Student stories and perspectives
  • Low-carbon and low-waste living

We also plan online campaigns and challenges that encourage young people to try practical environmental actions.

02

Student-Led Projects

Our aim is to support young people in creating and leading small sustainability projects that make sense for their own schools and communities.

  • Waste reduction and recycling initiatives
  • Zero-waste week challenges
  • Energy and water conservation campaigns
  • Green transport initiatives
  • Low-carbon lifestyle projects

These are examples of possible projects. They should not be read as completed S4C programmes.

03

Community Action

Our longer-term approach includes sustainability campaigns, community initiatives and workshops.

Before expanding those activities, S4C is focused on building an engaged community and collaborating thoughtfully with like-minded youth groups.

One phase at a time.

In our first phase, we are building posting habits, an initial audience and trusted relationships. In the next phase, that foundation can support eco-habit campaigns and sustainability initiatives.
